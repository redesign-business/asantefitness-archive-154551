# A Santé Lakeside Fitness — Demonstrated Proof

## Facility

- **9,000 square foot gym** located on the shores of Lake Tahoe in downtown Tahoe City, California
- Located just behind Safeway in Tahoe City
- Equipment includes: cardio machines, free weights, selectorized equipment
- Pilates studio on-site
- Amenities: locker rooms, showers, and a sauna
- Gym entrance photo available (source: homepage archive)

## Services Offered

- Group fitness classes (Cardio/Barre, Zumba, Yoga, Spin)
- Personal training (individual and buddy sessions)
- Daily, weekly, and monthly memberships
- 10-visit punch cards
- Drop-in day passes
- **All group fitness classes are free for members**
- 30-Day Challenge program — free monthly workout challenges posted online, no gym attendance required
- Outdoor training available with personal trainers
